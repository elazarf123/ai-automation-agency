The Problem

TechFlow's two Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) were drowning in a sea of unqualified leads. Each week, 400+ raw submissions piled up. Real buyers were buried under the noise. SDRs spent 10 hours each per week — 20 hours total — just triaging, never selling. Each lead required:

  • ✗ Checking company size and industry against ICP criteria
  • ✗ Verifying budget signals from the form submission
  • ✗ Cross-referencing LinkedIn to confirm seniority
  • ✗ Scoring and entering the lead into HubSpot manually
  • ✗ Writing a personalised first-touch email from scratch

The Solution

AutomateIQ built a custom AI lead-qualification agent that integrates directly with TechFlow's existing HubSpot CRM and Typeform lead-capture forms. Every new inbound lead is evaluated automatically — no human required for routine scoring.

1

Capture & Enrich

Every form submission triggers the agent. It pulls company data from Clearbit, verifies LinkedIn job title, and cross-checks industry against TechFlow's ICP definition — in under 3 seconds.

2

Score & Route

The AI scores each lead 0–100 on six dimensions: industry fit, company size, budget signals, decision-maker authority, urgency, and tech stack match. Grade A/B leads are auto-routed to the SDR queue in HubSpot with a priority flag.

3

Personalise & Notify

A personalised first-touch email is drafted — mentioning the prospect's specific industry pain points — and saved as a HubSpot email draft. A Slack message alerts the assigned SDR with the lead score and a one-line summary.

Technical Stack — roles in this build:

🤖 Claude AI (Anthropic)

Powers the lead-scoring engine — evaluating 6 ICP dimensions simultaneously and drafting personalised first-touch emails with contextual reasoning.

⚙️ Make.com

Orchestrates the end-to-end workflow — triggering on form submission, calling enrichment APIs, routing to HubSpot, and firing Slack alerts without code.

📋 HubSpot CRM

Receives enriched, scored lead records and email drafts. SDRs wake up to a prioritised queue — zero data entry required.

🔍 Clearbit + Typeform + Slack

Clearbit enriches company data; Typeform captures structured intake; Slack delivers instant SDR alerts with score summaries.

AI

Why Claude AI (Anthropic)?

Anthropic's Claude was selected for its ability to reason across ambiguous, unstructured data — the kind that fills real-world B2B lead forms. Unlike rule-based systems, Claude evaluates nuanced signals: a VP title buried in a long company description, implied urgency from phrasing, or a tech-stack mismatch hidden in a casual comment.

  • ✦ Multi-dimensional scoring — evaluates 6 ICP dimensions in a single API call
  • ✦ Contextual email drafting — personalises first-touch messages using lead-specific signals, not generic templates
  • ✦ Explainable output — returns a structured score with reasoning, so SDRs trust and act on the results
  • ✦ Safety-first API — Anthropic's Constitutional AI ensures outputs stay professional and on-brand
