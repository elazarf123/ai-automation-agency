The Problem

Vertex Analytics (name anonymised) is a B2B SaaS platform serving finance and operations teams. Their inbound pipeline was growing fast — which sounds like a good problem to have, until it's not.

With 500–600 leads flowing in each month from ads, content, and referrals, their two SDRs were spending over 20 hours per week on manual lead research and triage. The process was inconsistent — different reps scored leads differently — and high-quality enterprise prospects were sometimes sitting untouched for 3–4 days.

Their conversion rate from MQL to demo sat at 11%. Their VP of Sales knew the number should be higher; the team just didn't have bandwidth to focus on the right prospects.

⚙️

The Solution

We built a fully automated lead qualification pipeline using the Claude AI API and Make.com, integrated directly into their HubSpot CRM.

How It Works

  1. 1. New lead enters HubSpot from any source
  2. 2. AI enriches with company data & buying signals
  3. 3. Lead scored 0–100 across 8 fit criteria
  4. 4. Grade A/B/C automatically assigned in CRM
  5. 5. SDR receives Slack alert for Grade-A leads only

Scoring Criteria

  • ✓ Industry vertical fit
  • ✓ Company size & headcount
  • ✓ Estimated annual revenue
  • ✓ Job title / decision-making authority
  • ✓ Budget signals from enrichment
  • ✓ Urgency indicators in form data
  • ✓ Tech stack alignment
  • ✓ Current tool usage patterns

The entire pipeline from lead capture to scored HubSpot record takes under 90 seconds.

📅

Implementation Timeline

Week 1

Discovery & Setup
