About StyleHub Fashion

StyleHub Fashion is a direct-to-consumer e-commerce brand selling women's apparel and accessories online. With a growing SKU catalogue and a customer base that expects immediate answers, their support inbox had become a full-time problem requiring four dedicated staff members.

Most support tickets were repeating the same questions: "Where's my order?", "How do I return this?", "Do you have this in size M?" But with 2,000+ tickets per month and a 48-hour SLA that was slipping, customer reviews were trending negative.

The Problem

StyleHub's four-person support team handled over 2,000 tickets every month. Analysis showed that 70% of those tickets fell into just six categories:

  • ✗ Order status and tracking updates
  • ✗ Return and exchange policy questions
  • ✗ Size guide and fit recommendations
  • ✗ Discount code issues and promotional FAQs
  • ✗ Product availability and restock inquiries
  • ✗ Shipping options and delivery time estimates

Yet every one of these required a human to open the ticket, look up the answer, and compose a reply — a workflow that added up to $15,000/month in labour costs.

The Solution

AutomateIQ built a 24/7 AI support chatbot embedded on StyleHub's website and integrated with their Zendesk ticketing system. The bot handles Tier-1 (routine) queries immediately — only escalating to a human agent when the issue genuinely needs one.

1

Instant Answers

The chatbot is trained on StyleHub's full product catalogue, shipping policies, return procedures, and FAQs. It answers in seconds, 24/7 — no hold time, no queue. Order status is pulled live from Shopify.

2

Smart Escalation

When the AI detects frustration, a complex return dispute, or a question it can't confidently answer, it creates a Zendesk ticket pre-populated with the conversation history — so human agents have full context immediately.

3

Continuous Learning

Monthly review sessions analyse which questions the bot escalated — new patterns are used to update the knowledge base, so the bot gets smarter over time without constant manual maintenance.

Technical Spotlight — roles in this build:

🤖 Claude AI (Anthropic)

Powers context-aware, brand-consistent responses. Claude understands conversation history, detects customer frustration, and generates replies that feel human — not robotic — even for nuanced product questions.

⚙️ Make.com

Orchestrates real-time Shopify data retrieval for live order status and tracking. Make.com bridges the chatbot to Shopify's API, Zendesk ticketing, and Klaviyo — all without custom code.

🛒 Shopify API

Supplies real-time order status and tracking data, allowing the chatbot to answer "Where's my order?" accurately for every customer, every time.

🎫 Zendesk + Klaviyo + Slack

Zendesk receives pre-populated escalation tickets; Klaviyo syncs post-resolution follow-ups; Slack alerts the team on high-priority escalations.

🛡️

Safety Protocols — Smart Escalation Logic

Every client asks: "What if the AI gets it wrong?" That's why we built a multi-layer escalation system that ensures no customer falls through the cracks.

  • ✦ Frustration detection — Claude monitors tone; repeated negative phrasing triggers an immediate human handoff
  • ✦ Confidence thresholds — if Claude's confidence in a response falls below a set level, it escalates rather than guessing
  • ✦ Full context transfer — Zendesk tickets are pre-populated with the entire chat history so human agents never start from zero
  • ✦ Monthly audits — escalated patterns are reviewed and fed back into the knowledge base, continuously improving accuracy
