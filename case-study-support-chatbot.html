The Problem

NovaCrate (name anonymised) is a DTC e-commerce brand selling pet accessories online. With 8,000+ active customers and growing, their support inbox had become unmanageable.

Two support agents were fielding 350+ tickets per week, and roughly 70% were the same questions repeated over and over: "Where is my order?", "What's your return policy?", "Can I change my shipping address?", "Do you ship internationally?"

Average first response time had crept to 14 hours. CSAT scores sat at 3.4/5. Hiring a third support agent would have cost $3,500/month. The team was burning out, and the business was losing repeat customers due to poor support experiences.

⚙️

The Solution

We deployed a Claude-powered AI support chatbot trained on NovaCrate's full knowledge base — FAQs, returns policy, shipping info, product details — and integrated directly into their Zendesk queue and website widget.

How It Works

  1. 1. Customer submits ticket or opens chat widget
  2. 2. AI classifies intent from message content
  3. 3. For common queries: instant AI response (under 3 sec)
  4. 4. For order status: live lookup via Shopify API
  5. 5. Complex issues escalated to human with full context

What It Handles

  • ✓ Order status lookups (Shopify integration)
  • ✓ Returns & refunds policy questions
  • ✓ Shipping & delivery queries
  • ✓ Product recommendations & FAQs
  • ✓ Account & subscription management
  • ✓ Escalation to human with full chat transcript
  • ✓ 24/7 availability — evenings, weekends, holidays
📅

Implementation Timeline

Days 1–3

Knowledge Base Audit

Reviewed 6 months of support tickets to identify top-50 question categories. Mapped escalation logic. Exported existing FAQs and policy docs.

Days 4–9

Build & Integration

Trained Claude on knowledge base, built Shopify order lookup, integrated with Zendesk and website chat widget. Set up escalation routing and handoff flow.

Days 10–14

Testing & Go-Live

Shadow-mode testing for 5 days — AI handled queries in parallel with humans for quality review. Tone tuned to match brand voice. Full go-live on Day 14.
