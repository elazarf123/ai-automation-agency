      Frequently Asked Questions

      Can it integrate with HubSpot or Salesforce?
      Yes — we integrate directly with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive. When a lead is scored, the result is written back to your CRM as a custom property, and high-scoring leads can automatically trigger tasks, sequences, or notifications for your sales team. No manual data entry, no switching tabs.
      How do we customise the scoring criteria for our business?
      During the audit we map your ideal customer profile — the industries, company sizes, budget ranges, and signals that matter most for your sales team. That becomes the scoring rubric we build into the AI. You can refine the criteria any time, and we include two rounds of tuning in every build so the scoring matches what your best reps would do.
      What does it cost to set up and maintain?
      Setup is included in our build fee. Ongoing maintenance costs are minimal — typically $20–$50/month in AI API calls for teams scoring up to 1,000 leads/month. Our monthly retainer covers updates, monitoring, and tuning. See the full breakdown on our pricing page.
