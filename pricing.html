What's Actually Included

No surprises. Here's exactly what you get at each tier — and why we price things the way we do.

🎯 Lead Qualification Agent

We build a custom AI that reads every inbound lead, scores them 0–100 against your ideal customer profile, and routes Grade-A and B leads directly to your CRM with a personalised first-touch email draft. The agent is trained on your ICP, your product, and your sales motion — not a generic template.

Why it costs what it does: Custom ICP training, CRM integration, email personalisation engine, and ongoing monthly tuning as your market evolves. This replaces 1–2 SDR hours per working day.

📄 Invoice Processing Agent

We build a pipeline that captures invoices from any source (email, upload, scan), extracts all data using AI OCR, validates against your purchase orders, posts directly into your accounting system, and flags exceptions for human review. Works with PDFs, images, and Word documents.

Why it costs what it does: Multi-format document processing, PO matching logic, exception-handling workflows, and direct accounting system integration (QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite). Replaces 60–120 hours/month of AP data entry.

💬 Support Chatbot Agent

We train a 24/7 chatbot on your product docs, FAQs, policies, and support history. It resolves Tier-1 questions instantly and escalates complex issues to human agents with full conversation context. Integrates with your helpdesk so your existing workflow stays intact.

Why it costs what it does: Knowledge base training, helpdesk integration, smart escalation logic, live product data feeds (e.g., order status from Shopify), and monthly knowledge base updates as your product evolves.

Pricing FAQ

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fees. The monthly subscription covers the full build, onboarding, integration, and first-month operation. You start paying after we've completed the setup and the automation is live.

Can I start on Starter and upgrade later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with one automation to validate results, then upgrade to Growth once they see the ROI. Upgrades take effect on your next billing cycle and we handle the migration.

What happens if I exceed my volume limit on Starter?

We'll reach out to discuss upgrading your plan. We don't cut off your automation mid-month — we'll keep things running and sort it out together. Over-volume is charged at $0.10 per additional item if you prefer to stay on Starter temporarily.

How long does implementation take?

Starter plan agents typically go live in 2–3 weeks. Growth plan (3 agents) typically takes 3–4 weeks because we're building and integrating multiple systems simultaneously. Enterprise timelines are scoped per project.

Do I own the automation when I cancel?

The Make.com workflows and configurations belong to your account. If you cancel, we provide a full documentation handover and help you transition to in-house management if desired. We want clients to stay because they love the results — not because they're locked in.
