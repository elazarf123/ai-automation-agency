Frequently Asked Questions

Can it integrate with our existing helpdesk (Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk)?
Yes — we connect directly to Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, and any platform with an API. Your team keeps working in the same tools they already use. The AI agent sits in front of your helpdesk and only passes tickets through when escalation is needed. Setup takes a few hours, not weeks.
What happens when the bot doesn't know the answer?
The agent is trained to recognise the limits of its knowledge. When it encounters a question outside its scope, it tells the customer it's escalating to a specialist, then immediately creates a ticket or pings your team in Slack. No customer gets stuck in a dead end. We also review escalations weekly to continuously improve coverage.
How much does it cost to run after we launch?
Beyond our monthly retainer, running costs are typically $30–$80/month in AI API calls for a mid-sized business handling 2,000–5,000 conversations per month. We optimise prompts and caching to keep costs low. Full details are on our pricing page.
